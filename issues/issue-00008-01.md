Analysis. Cause: (1) shared/css/page.css gives a fit cell max-width:100% and base pack.css gives .mkd-hstack min-width:0, so a fit frame is squeezed to the gallery card's width and row children shrink under their content — the variant rows of Button, ControlGroup, DatePicker, Form, Gauge, List, Picker, TabView and Toggle overlap (SRC-9#images, SRC-9#scroll). (2) base pack.css .mkd-layer is position:absolute; inset:0, so at fit the screen has no size of its own and Alert, ConfirmationDialog, Dialog, Popover, Sheet, Toast and Tooltip collapse to a small square. (3) MessageBubble text has no max-width/wrap. Spec pages: REND.2 (R-DEV-008), REND.1 (R-REND-013), WEB.2 (R-WEB-004.C13, C15). Violated: F-8.C3/C5 and F-18.AC2 intent; the fix adds R-DEV-008 and R-REND-013. Proposed fix: fit frames at max-content width with the cell scrolling horizontally; presentation layers in flow at fit (frame ≥ surface + padding); non-text row children flex-shrink:0 at min-content, text wraps/truncates; bubble max-width 75%; gallery geometry assertions in gallery.spec.mjs.
