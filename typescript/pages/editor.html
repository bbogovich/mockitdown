Editor and outline elements

<mockitdown-editor> renders a mockup in edit mode. Click an element to select it; click it again or press Esc to select its parent, Enter to go back; Alt-click cycles through the elements under the pointer. A row of the ForEach selects the ForEach and shows a "derived" badge.

<mockitdown-outline> shows the same session's element tree. Click a row to select it; ↑ ↓ move, → ← expand and collapse, Home and End jump, and typing selects the next row whose name starts with the letters.
