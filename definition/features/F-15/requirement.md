G1: Documentation can point at parts of a mockup and walk a reader through a workflow.
C1: Numbered callouts attach to any element, with an optional label, in a colour of the author's choice.
C2: The same numbers written in the surrounding prose render as matching circles.
C3: Pointers, taps, drag-select rectangles and focus highlights can be drawn on or around elements.
C4: Marks can also be placed freely on a canvas.
AC1: A walkthrough page shows a mockup with callouts 1–4 and prose referring to (#1)–(#4) with matching circles.
AC2: Marks are not clipped by the lists or cards that contain the marked elements.
C5: Callouts and other marks attached to an overlay — a dialog, a toast or a floating action button — are drawn as on any other element.
C6: Marks on elements inside a dialog are placed at those elements, not at a corner of the screen.
AC3: A dialog and a toast with callouts show them, and a callout on a button inside a dialog sits at that button's corner.
